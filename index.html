Methodology & sources · as of

Sources

Categories

Teal remote-friendly: no fixed minimum, or 0–1 days. Amber hybrid: 2–3 days. Red office-first: 4–5 days. Grey not confirmed: we found no reputable public source for the London policy.

Scope

"Company-wide" means the source describes a firm-wide policy and we found no reported London exception. Where reporting covered US staff only, the London policy is marked not confirmed.

Estimates

Office locations are approximate, at building level. Building heights are stylised. The Monday-to-Friday lights and commuter trails are an illustrative model based on each policy, not measured occupancy or TfL data.

Limits

Web search was unavailable when this edition was compiled. Policies come only from pages we could open: official memos and careers pages, and reputable outlets. Anything else is grey. Summaries are paraphrased, with no direct quotes. Corrections are welcome.
